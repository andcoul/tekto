There are 23 clips: 17 exercises and 6 screen heroes. The pack also has **photo edits** that recast the existing photos. Each one turns a Tekto photo into a short seamless loop. The casting follows `specs/001-tekto-ai-mvp/research.md` §16.

## How to use

1. **Pick a tool.** Use any image-to-video or text-to-video tool, such as Kling, Runway, Google Veo or Sora.
2. **Start image (optional).** Export the matching still from Figma: page "Brand, components & imagery", section Imagery. Select the rectangle, then Export as PNG at 2x.
   - The current stills don't match the new casting yet, because they are regenerated once Figma is available. Until then, use **text-to-video**: each prompt describes the full scene.
3. **Paste the prompt.** For each clip, paste the **Base style** followed by the clip's prompt. Add the **Negative prompt** if the tool has a field for it.
4. **Settings:**
   - 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920 if possible (at least 720×1280).
   - 5 seconds (4–6 is fine).
   - 24 or 30 fps.
   - Lowest motion or camera-movement setting, with the camera locked.
5. **Save** as MP4 (H.264) with the exact **file name** shown, then send them all to me.
6. **Check before sending.** Same person throughout, the loop's first and last frames match, form looks correct, and there's no text or logo.

Use this section to make the **photos** with the new casting in any image tool (for example ChatGPT, Midjourney or Ideogram).

Fitted gym clothing in black, charcoal or slate grey. Matte black equipment. Single athlete, sharp focus, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no yellow, no red.

## Base style (paste first, every time)

> Cinematic vertical 9:16 fitness footage, photoreal, shot on a 35mm lens at chest height, locked-off tripod camera with no camera movement. A dark, moody premium gym: near-black charcoal background (#0F1014) with soft haze. Lighting is a warm key light on the athlete's face and body, plus a cool pale-blue rim light (#9DB8FF) outlining the silhouette, keeping deep skin tones rich, detailed and well exposed against the dark background. Fitted gym clothing in black, charcoal or slate grey. Matte black equipment. Calm, controlled, slow tempo. Seamless loop: the final frame matches the first frame. Single athlete, no other people. No text, no logos, no brand marks, no watermarks.

## Negative prompt

> text, captions, logos, brand names, watermark, yellow, red, neon, extra limbs, extra fingers, distorted hands, warped barbell, bending bar, morphing weights, face changing, camera shake, zoom, cuts, crowd, mirror reflections, rounded lower back, knees caving inward, jerky motion, slow motion blur, overexposed skin, underexposed face
